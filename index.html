How It Works

Three moving parts. Nothing else.

No need to stare at the chart all day. Enjoy your freedom. Set an alert and forget it — every decision the indicator makes is visible on your chart, the moment it happens.

01

A / A+ Signals

Every breakout that clears the trend and momentum filters prints an A Buy/Sell. When it's also moving against the 1-hour trend — a higher-conviction setup — it prints A+ instead.

02

Target & Invalidation

The moment a signal fires, a target and a stop are calculated from that candle's high or low — no discretion, no moving the goalposts mid-trade.

03

Live Scoreboard

Entries, TP hits, SL hits, and your last 3 trade outcomes — updating on your chart in real time. The numbers, not a story about the numbers.

What markets and timeframes does it work on?

Built and tested on MNQ and NDX futures. Signals print on the 5, 15, 20, 25, and 30-minute timeframes.
